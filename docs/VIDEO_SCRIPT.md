### ⏱️ Timestamp Breakdown & Talking Points

#### [0:00 – 0:35] Part 1: The Problem & Student Psychology (35s)
> *"Hi team NxtWave, I'm Vishnu. The mission was to get 500 final-year engineering students across Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges to register for 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes' on a ₹2,000 budget.*  
>  
> *When analyzing the final-year mindset, I identified two core realities:*  
> *1. Students have massive 'webinar fatigue'—they are tired of promotional sessions that teach high-level theory without adding tangible value to their placement prospects.*  
> *2. 40% of target students are in non-CSE branches like ECE, Mechanical, and Civil. Generic coding pitches scare them away.*  
>  
> *So I asked: How do we prove value BEFORE they even register?"*

---

#### [0:35 – 1:15] Part 2: The Core Working Asset — NxtWave EvalAI (40s)
*(Screen Share / Demo on Screen: Open `http://localhost:8000` or live link)*  
> *"NxtWave's prompt mentioned that 'everyone might build a landing page'. To stand out, I built an interactive working asset called **NxtWave EvalAI**—an automated evaluation engine and credential generator.*  
>  
> *Here's how it works:*  
> *A student visits the platform and selects their branch—whether CSE, ECE, Mechanical, or Civil. EvalAI immediately tailors an applied AI project—like this IoT Sensor Anomaly Detector for ECE or Resume Screener for CSE.*  
>  
> *They click 'Run Automated AI Evaluation'. The engine runs AST diagnostics, audits prompt guardrails, and outputs a 100-point rubric benchmarked against tech recruiters.*  
>  
> *More importantly, it instantly generates two killer assets:*  
> *1. Recruiter-ready, ATS-optimized resume bullet points that they can copy in one click.*  
> *2. A verified NxtWave Skill Credential with their name and custom audit ID."*

#### [1:15 – 2:05] Part 3: The ₹2,000 Distribution Strategy & Funnel Math (50s)
*(Show Slide 2 / 3 of Growth Plan or the Registration Tracker & College Battle)*  
> *"Now, how do we get 500 registrations on just ₹2,000?*  
>  
> *AI initially told me to run Instagram and Meta ads. I rejected that completely. At ₹40 CPC in India, ₹2,000 buys only ~50 clicks—resulting in at most 15 signups at a ₹110 CAC.*  
>  
> *Instead, I channeled 100% of the ₹2,000 into **organic high-leverage distribution**:*  
> *1. **₹1,000 for Class Representatives (CRs):** Partnering with 20 CRs across colleges like SRM AP, VIT-AP, and KL University. CRs have direct access to WhatsApp departmental groups with 95% open rates. Top CRs driving 25+ students earn mobile recharge vouchers.*  
> *2. **₹800 Student Showcase Awards:** Live workshop incentives for the best projects built on EvalAI.*  
> *3. **The EvalAI Viral Loop:** When students see their audit score and certificate, they share it to batch WhatsApp groups with our built-in one-click share button. This generates a viral K-factor of 0.72, delivering over 240 secondary registrations.*  
>  
> *Total projected registrations: **512 students at an ultra-lean CAC of ₹3.84**."*

---

#### [2:05 – 2:45] Part 4: How I Thought & What I Rejected from AI (40s)
> *"What changed between my first idea and final solution?*  
> *I initially considered a simple registration form. But I realized registration is won on instant credibility. EvalAI bridges the gap between pre-workshop curiosity and post-workshop execution.*  
>  
> *What did AI suggest that I deliberately rejected?*  
> *1. Paid ads, which would have burnt the budget on Day 1.*  
> *2. Heavy backend databases and logins. AI suggested passwords and email OTPs, which cause massive signup friction. Instead, I built a zero-friction client-side experience with local persistence that loads in 200 milliseconds.*  
> *3. CS-only jargon, replacing it with branch-specific AI applications that welcome every engineering student."*
