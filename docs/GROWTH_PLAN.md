## 📑 Slide 1 / Page 1: Understand the Student

### 1. Who Exactly Are We Targeting?
* **Core Segment:** Final-Year B.Tech / B.E. Students (Batch of 2025 & 2026) across Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges in AP & Telangana (e.g., SRM University AP, VIT-AP, KL University, Vignan, JNTU affiliates, CBIT, Vasavi).
* **Branch Mix:**
  * **60% CSE / IT / AI-DS:** Facing intense competition; recruiters reject standard academic projects (basic CRUD, simple weather apps).
  * **40% Non-CSE (ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil):** High placement anxiety due to shrinking core hiring; eager to pivot to tech/AI roles but intimidated by heavy machine learning theory.
* **Student Mindset & Pain Points:**
  * **The "Useless Webinar" Fatigue:** Final-year students are overwhelmed by promotional webinars that teach high-level theory without providing tangible resume value.
  * **Placement Proof Dilemma:** Recruiters demand verifiable AI projects, but students don't know how to deploy an LLM app or write ATS-friendly resume bullets.

### 2. Why Would They Care?
* **Zero-Theory Output:** Walk in with zero AI background $\rightarrow$ Walk out with a working, deployed AI project on GitHub in 60 minutes.
* **Instant Verifiable Credential:** Through **NxtWave EvalAI**, every student's project code is automatically audited against industry standards, generating a **Verifiable Skill Credential** and recruiter-ready ATS bullets.
* **Branch Relevance:** Tailored project tracks (e.g., *AI Resume Screener* for CSE; *IoT Sensor Anomaly Detector* for ECE; *Predictive Maintenance AI* for Mechanical).

### 3. What Triggers Immediate Registration?
1. **The "Resume Gap" Solution:** A direct answer to campus placement interviews.
2. **Instant Pre-Workshop Value (EvalAI):** Students can test sample project code and see their potential certificate *before* the workshop even begins.
3. **Peer Social Proof & College Pride:** Inter-college leaderboard showing their campus battle against rival universities.
